## 1. Wby Jira and DotProject

In the 1990s started developing and delivering business and marketing plans for customers and partners (including startups from scratch).

During COVID, studied Jira- which was then useful when called on a mission were, eventually, was also to deliver a Jira training to a multinational remote team.

As for DotProject: local commentary was that nobody used it- but, frankly, in the 2000s was pivotal in my support to startups via [PartnershipIncubator.com](https://partnershipincubator.com).

I used to replicated what had designed as processed to support a customer in the 1990s using Lotus Notes Workgroup, i.e. a kind of repurposing of the "discussion" database into a wiki to generate a critical mass before unleashing meetings with key people.

With DotProject, as managed also a funnel to filter in/out partners, on each project I had also one or more discussion groups that enabled e.g. quick onboarding of new team members, as one of the discussion groups in each initiative containing a kind of "You Are Here and This Where We Are".

As coordinator, facilitator, and project/program/change manager but with a background in controlling, financial controlling, and management reporting, I am used to generate "information packages".

Hence, asked Claude to help extract the current status, integrate with a "shipping manifest" (my commentary)- all within SQLite3, so that I can then integrate it with other pipelines where I do analysis, presentation, etc.

## 2. Why a MorningNewsDashboard

Since the late 1980s, was used to integrate whatever produced within dashboard, e.g. Executive Information Systems, with an audience target that was generally CXX, but "linking" to those able to provide background on each information presented.

My role in my business network in the 1990s and 2000s included keeping up-to-date about cross-domain evolution and trends, including by looking at news and research continuously.

So, one of my first "semi-automation", "human in the loop" experiments that shared online was to actually "train" an agents team and a coordinator, plus some ancillary agents, so that they could do in minutes everyday (now twice a day) something that took me hours.

Moreover, it is still listed as an "experiments", as I keep evolving structure and content, but now is converging toward a stabilization.

Hence, decided that was the right time to create a dashboard based on that public content ([visit the README.md](https://github.com/robertolofaro/drafting-with-claude/tree/master/README.md)).

You will find that also designed, and asked Claude to draft, a basic token management system, to allow differentiating between a free and a paid service- but using "daily tokens".

For some activities, will be integrated with local models- from classification to actual smaller LLMs (my standard also on [HuggingFace](https://hf.co/robertolofaro) is a 4B variant of Qwen).

## 3. Why StratBPR

In the early 1990s, after smaller activities in cultural and organizational change and management training curricula development and delivery, was hired as Senior Project Manager, and after one month assigned to develop a business unit in that area.

In 1993, had my first multi-year assignment as program manager in that area, and decided to "invest" more than before (as I already purchased books and read material on any industry or domain I worked in- no Internet back then).

Which included looking around at organizational change initiatives, but not academic- practical.

I was lucky enough that "re-inventing the government" was then alive and kicking, and it was pre-9/11, i.e. a time when the USA Government still shared information to influence.

So, ended up getting some free subscription ( ([visit the README.md](https://github.com/robertolofaro/drafting-with-claude/tree/master/README.md)), including a CD containing both Federal procurement rules, a library on Business Process Re-engineering, and a software called TurboBPR.

The software had many characteristics (see the subdirectory to have more information), including assessment, generating and comparing scenarios, analysis, and producing documentation.

Hence, in my AI experiments since ChatGPT went online in 2022, routinely that element was part of my "portfolio".

I was working on other variants, integrating also processes that covered for partners since the 1990s (e.g. the "Devil's Advocate" role- on projects, contracts, suppliers, startups, business&marketing plans, budget, and all the business paraphernalia you can think of- e.g. attacking a new proposed business case or product).

It seemed just appropriate to provide the same instructions I had given to others also to Claude, to see how would create a first draft.
