# drafting-with-claude


## Rationale

As the title of this repository says: this is "DraftingWithClaude"- and the aim is to give you a starting point, as I got, and develop your own.

I only ask a favor: please CC-BY-SA-4.0- i.e. share the link to this repository and the source, so that others too can see the rationale, original content, and decide how to proceed.

That said: the CC-BY-SA-4.0 allows also commercial uses, i.e. you can create your own application also to sell a service to others (or for your internal corporate uses.

Reason? I am tired of receiving on Linkedin "breaking news" announces of what are frankly re-hashing of free material that I already saw in training courses, YouTube videos, and other free sources, all presented as if were new services or products, and never quoting the source.

So, the help I received, including the suggestion to share with others that could benefit from it, since the early 1990s (first from the most uncommong source- the USA Department of Defense), was an inspiration for this repository and sharing my journey.

The next step, after these experiments in the first half of October 2026? Follow step-by-step the training plan that I can concocted with different AIs (Claude included).

I think that idea-generation is now almost free, but it is the steering and services that deliver the value-added.

Software per se is not where the value will be- keeping the software usable, up-to-date, aligned to business needs, and providing a responsive service will be what will drive customers to you.

Hence, I am working on my own versions for my own purposes, but you are welcome.

Within the "TableOfContent.md" I enclose a short summary of what each folder (and application draft contained therein) are for.

Enjoy!

PS and feed-back [via private messages on Linkedin](https://linkedin.com/in/robertolofaro) is welcome.

## History

October 2026 is the month that decided to use to structure my knowledge about Claude, and probably will do an encore routinely, as for most of my information processing and code generation I will keep using local models- with current models, it takes longer, but the quality is comparable, while keeping data privacy, confidentiality (unless you have Trojan malware on your computer), and easier to integrate in pipelines (I use an approach that first learned in the 1980s using a scheduling system on IBM Mainframes called UCC7, that was eventually acquired by [Computer Associates and renamed CA7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CA-7_(software))).

My approach, as I followed previous training and used different AIs since few years, has been to first identify "case studies" where I prepared the "scope" of each request to Claude, and have it, in as few interactions as possible, generate different tools.

Sometimes re-inventing tools that I had already started to rebuild following tools that are not available anymore (e.g. [TurboBPR](https://www.govexec.com/magazine/1996/09/turbobpr-speeds-pentagon-projects/1258/), which was contained in a CD that received in the early 1990s from the then-USA DoD, as part of the "re-inventing the government" mailing list subscription and expression of interest, along with a subscription to the magazine [CrossTalk](https://crosstalk.nps.edu/), a magazine really on adopting a systemic/cross-domain perspective in software development, for defense/regulated industry software engineering).

And sometimes using my own material to generate new tools.


## Limitations
