# Why this folder

As wrote within the README.md of this repository, the purpose of the repository is to share part of my journey to do systematically in a couple of weeks a Claude learning path.

This was done in October 2026, by using a monthly Claude Pro subscription.

Before starting, actually with Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, and local models worked on a plan for a workshop to be carried out in two weeks.

In reality the first two weeks of the months were used to try to apply prior Claude and post-2021 AI experience.

Or: generative, LLMs, and associated- not the traditional Machine Learning or what learned and used in the 1980s-1990s.

I selected existing processes, application, concepts I had worked on in the past, and asked ClaudePro using Sonnet 5.5 to develop manuals and/or applications.

The projects that shared in this repository are a blend of small and smaller, those that I used for the test were more of less of the same type.

The project types? 

From preparing material for training workshops on various subjects (including conceptual- e.g. learning Inkscape and GIMP for my own publication purposes), to software refactoring (i.e. providing manuals or existing software, and asking to prepare new versions).

Then, on 2026-10-19 switched to Opus 5.5, and asked to review each one of those dossiers, to see how were consumed "tokens" (really: the session and weekly budget).

To be practical: I saw that around three of those projects absorb the full budget of a ClaudePro session and take around 20-30mins each, coming up with around 1/3 of the weekly budget.

The two-weeks workshop has two parts:
* Ecosystem- what is within Claude and how and when to blend which tools in which environment
* Engineering with Claude- using Claude to refactor or to integrate legacy hardware.

The latter could surprise some- as I have more modern SOCs and processors available- but the purpose is to do what has been customary in real business activities: e.g. look around, and ATMs still use Windows 95 or 98, often.

Practical example: when Estonia became independent, had to built everything from scratch- the first e-government nation, in the 1990s.

Thirty years later, what was built sometimes "fast and furious" is now legacy- and, while in the past they could set an example of digital transformation, now have the unique opportunity of showing digital transition, as they too have plenty of legacy- but going back to the 1990s, not the 1950s.

Also the workshop material was subject to the same approach: and asked on 2026-10-19 (completed 2026-10-20) Claude Opus 5.5 to review and suggest improvements on the workshop material.

I will release the material gradually, only once I have personally used, but generally you can expect:
* templates: to help learning, but also to learn tracking your projects and experiments and Claude behavioral patterns (as Claude itself states that change on a weekly basis)
* training manual: the starting point is a couple of dozen pages for both worshop parts, but will integrated with my notes
* exercises: provided by the various AIs, and finally reviewed by Claude Opus 5.5 on 2026-10-20, along with test scripts
* solutions: ditto
* visual maps: will release using Mermaid (PNG + MMD source if I will have to use my non-standard diagrams such as Waterfall, Bubble Chart, Feynman [accessible here](https://robertolofaro.com/diagrams_tool.php))

* Hope that the material will be useful to some- at least, to start conceptually.
